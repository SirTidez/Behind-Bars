01 / In the current mod

The single-player justice loop is the active build.

Behind Bars expands what happens after an arrest: serving time, getting out, returning on parole, and keeping that history connected to the player.

Jail and release

Arrest through release

Booking, custody, sentence tracking, bail, jail doors, guards, inmates, and release-officer flows are the core single-player systems under development.

Parole

Follow-up after release

Parole conditions, check-ins, violations, officer coordination, and completion are in active development alongside the jail lifecycle.

Crime and records

Consequences that carry forward

Crime, witness, rap-sheet, and parole-record paths are connected to the wider justice flow.

Game support

Schedule I v0.4.6f13

The current beta environment uses MelonLoader 0.7.0. Check the player guide again after a Schedule I update.
